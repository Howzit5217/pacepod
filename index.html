PacePod

A little speedometer that floats over Pikmin Bloom while you ride, and tells you when to slow down or speed up.

Download for Android

Version 1.0.4 · all versions

Floats over your game

Sits over Pikmin Bloom, or any app you pick, and gets out of the way when you leave it.

Colors at a glance

Green while you’re fine, yellow near your top speed, red past it, and yellow again when you’re riding too slowly.

Alerts you hear or feel

A buzz, one of four sounds or your own, from the phone or Bluetooth, and a buzz on your Galaxy Watch.

Set it your way

mph or km/h, 0, 0.0 or 0.00, how see-through it is, and an Eco mode that saves battery.

In ten languages

The same as Pikmin Bloom: English, Español, Français, Português, Deutsch, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어, 繁體中文 and 简体中文.

Updates itself

New versions come from this page by themselves, while the pill is out of the way. Nothing to download again.

Private

Your location stays on your phone. The only thing PacePod fetches is its own updates, from this page.

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android and open PacePod.apk when it’s done.
  2. If Android asks, let your browser install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open PacePod. A small card walks you through what it needs: location, showing over other apps, and (to pop up with Pikmin Bloom) its switch in Accessibility.

“Restricted setting”? Android blocks the Accessibility switch for apps from outside the Play Store until you allow it: Settings › Apps › PacePod › ⋮ (top corner) › Allow restricted settings. Then turn the switch on again.

It updates itself. Every few hours PacePod checks this page, and puts in a new version by itself once the pill is out of the way. The first time, long-press the pill and tap Allow next to Updates, so Android lets it. If Google Play Protect stops to check a new version, let it install.

On version 1.0.3 or older? Install this one over it once: your settings stay, and from then on it updates itself.
