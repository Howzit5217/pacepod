PacePod

A little speedometer that floats over Pikmin Bloom while you ride, and tells you when to slow down or speed up.

Download for Android iPhone and computers

Version 1.0.0 · all versions

What it does

  • A glassy speed pill, like the game’s own buttons, that sits over Pikmin Bloom (or any app you pick) and gets out of the way when you leave.
  • Green while you’re fine, yellow as you near your top speed, red past it, and yellow again if you’re riding slower than you’d like.
  • Speed alerts you can hear or feel: a buzz, one of four sounds or your own, and a buzz on your Galaxy Watch, so you don’t have to look.
  • Choose mph or km/h and 0, 0.0 or 0.00. Eco mode saves battery.
  • Your location stays on your phone. PacePod doesn’t send it anywhere.

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android and open PacePod.apk when it’s done.
  2. If Android asks, let your browser install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open PacePod. A small card walks you through what it needs: location, showing over other apps, and (to pop up with Pikmin Bloom) its switch in Accessibility.

“Restricted setting”? Android blocks the Accessibility switch for apps from outside the Play Store until you allow it: Settings › Apps › PacePod › ⋮ (top corner) › Allow restricted settings. Then turn the switch on again.

Updating? Install the new version over the old one; your settings stay.

iPhone and computers

  1. Open PacePod for the web in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen with its own icon, like an app.
  3. Tap Start and allow location.

On iPhone it can’t float. Apple doesn’t let any app or web page sit on top of other apps, and Safari stops reading your speed once you switch away. So on iPhone PacePod is a full-screen speedometer, with the same pill, colours and alerts, and it keeps your screen on while it’s open. Buzzing needs Android; on iPhone the alerts are sounds.
